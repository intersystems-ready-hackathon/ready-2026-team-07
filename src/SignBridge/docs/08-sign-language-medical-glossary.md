# Sign-Language Medical Glossary — Cues for Interpreters

**Category:** glossary
**Condition:** sign_cues
**Red flag:** false

This glossary lists core medical concepts likely to appear in a primary-care or emergency visit, along with a brief plain-English explanation and a generic sign cue suitable for ASL. Regional sign styles (BSL, PSL, etc.) may differ — interpreters should adapt.

## Anatomy

- **Heart** — fist over left chest, two pulses.
- **Lungs** — open hands on upper chest, breath motion.
- **Kidney** — fingertip touch to lower back, both sides.
- **Brain** — index finger taps temple.
- **Stomach** — circular motion over abdomen.
- **Blood** — index finger taps lips, then "B" handshape moves down.

## Symptoms

- **Pain** — index fingers twist toward each other near affected area.
- **Sharp pain** — pain sign with quick, precise motion.
- **Dull pain** — pain sign with slow, weighted motion.
- **Pressure** — flat hand pushes down on the chest.
- **Shortness of breath** — open hand fans rapidly in front of the mouth.
- **Dizziness** — index finger circles near temple.
- **Nausea** — open hand circles over stomach with grimace.
- **Fever** — back of hand to forehead with raised eyebrows.

## Procedures and tests

- **Blood pressure** — squeezing motion on opposite arm.
- **ECG / EKG** — finger traces zigzag across chest.
- **X-ray** — flat hand passes in front of body, light flash.
- **Ultrasound** — flat hand glides over abdomen with wave motion.
- **MRI** — fingers flick outward (magnetic field), hands trace tube shape.
- **Blood draw** — pinch fingers on inner elbow, then pulling motion.
- **IV** — index finger into back of opposite hand, then drip motion above.

## Conditions

- **Heart attack** — heart sign + sharp jolt motion.
- **Stroke** — index finger to side of head, then collapse to one side.
- **Diabetes** — sweet sign (index taps chin) + sick sign (middle finger to forehead).
- **High blood pressure** — blood-pressure sign + raise hands upward.
- **Asthma** — chest sign + tight squeeze motion.

## Medications and units

- **Pill** — pinched fingers to mouth.
- **Injection** — thumb-press into opposite arm.
- **Inhaler** — fist to mouth with quick puff.
- **Daily** — index finger sweeps across the day.
- **Twice a day** — same as daily, with two tap.
- **As needed** — open hand shakes side to side, "when necessary."

## Time and urgency

- **Now** — flat hands push down twice in front of body.
- **Soon** — F-handshape near mouth, tight motion.
- **Emergency** — E-handshape circles chest with urgent expression.
